One app from the first call to the final invoice

  • CEC 2024 built in

    Calculators and drawing checks follow CSA C22.1:24 with provincial amendments. NEC 2023 is there if a job needs it.

  • Tools for the field

    Wire runs measured with AR, conduit bends with marks, box layouts on the plan. They work offline and sync when you're back in range.

  • Estimate to invoice

    Take off from the drawing, price it, send the quote, turn it into a job and bill it with the right GST, HST or PST.

  • Crew and time

    Plan the crew's day, clock in on a shared iPad with a PIN, and see hours by job before payroll.

  • AI that drafts, you approve

    The assistant writes estimates, invoices and follow-ups from your notes. Nothing goes out until you approve it.
