Services

We'll set it up with you

Fishtape is built to set up yourself, with a guide for every step. If you'd rather not spend an evening on DNS records and API keys, we'll do it with you.

  • Done-for-you setup

    We connect your accounts with you on a screen share, test each one with a real payment, email or text, and show your office how it works.

    • Stripe: card payments on invoices, tested with a real payment and refund
    • SendGrid domain authentication, so your quotes don't land in spam
    • Twilio: a local number for texts, with STOP handled
    • Facebook Lead Ads: your lead forms straight into the pipeline

    Quoted per job, up front. Included with Enterprise.

    Talk to us

  • Data import from your old software

    Send us the exports from Jobber, QuickBooks Online, Housecall Pro or your spreadsheets. We bring clients, the price book, jobs and employees across and check them with you.

    • Clients with addresses, tags and notes
    • Your price book with costs, sell prices and labour hours
    • Open jobs and your people

    Quoted per job.

    Talk to us

  • Price book build-out

    A price book that matches how you quote: materials from your supplier's list, your labour units, assemblies for the things you install every week.

    • Materials by category with your costs and markup
    • Labour hours per device
    • Units set up so wire runs price by the foot, metre or coil

    Quoted per job.

    Talk to us

  • Crew training, remote or on site

    Get the office and the crew using it the same week: the time clock and My day for the field, estimates and invoices for the office.

    • Remote sessions by video, recorded for new hires
    • On site at your shop (travel quoted)
    • A short guide for your own way of working

    Quoted per job.

    Talk to us

  • Custom plan sets and templates

    Your symbols, layers, room defaults and task templates set up once, so every drawing and work plan starts the way you like it.

    • Symbol and layer sets for your drawings
    • Placement defaults: mounting heights, switch distance, alarms
    • Work-plan task templates and SOPs

    Quoted per job.

  • Enterprise

    For bigger shops: as many users as you need, single sign-on, setup done for you and a contact who knows your account.

    • Custom user counts
    • Single sign-on
    • Setup, import and training included
