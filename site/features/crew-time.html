Crew and time

Hours by job, without chasing timesheets

The crew clocks in on the van's iPad with their PIN, against the job they're on. Breaks are tracked, timesheets approved weekly, and the hours land on the job cost.

Included in: Every plan Compare plans

Start free trial See pricing

What it does

  • Shared iPad, own PINRegister the iPad once. Each person signs in with their name and PIN, locked out after too many wrong tries.
  • Clock in to the jobIn and out against a job, breaks on and off, and who's on the clock right now.
  • TimesheetsWeekly timesheets, corrections, manager approval and a CSV export for payroll.
  • People recordsRoles, licences and certifications with expiry dates, PINs and what each role can see.

How it works on site

  1. Add your people with their role and a PIN.
  2. Register the shared iPad from a manager's account.
  3. Crew clock in to the job in the morning. It works without signal and syncs later.
  4. Approve the week's timesheets and export them for payroll.

Which plan includes it

Every plan has no caps on invoices, estimates, jobs, clients or drawings. Plans differ by features and included users.

  • Time clockEvery plan
  • EmployeesEvery plan
  • Crew scheduleEvery plan

Good to know. Owners, admins and HQ staff sign in with a password and two-step codes; crew on a shared iPad use their PIN, limited to that device.
