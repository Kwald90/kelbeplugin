Features / iPad and iPhone app

iPad and iPhone

Made for the iPad in the truck

Fishtape runs full-screen on the iPad and iPhone, works in a basement with no signal, and syncs when you're back in range. Apple Pencil on the drawings, big buttons for gloved hands.

Included in: Every plan Compare plans

Start free trial See pricing

What it does

  • Works offlineTime clock, drawings, calculators, bending, wire runs, SOPs and today's jobs work without a connection. Changes sync in the background.
  • Install from Safari todayOpen Fishtape in Safari and Add to Home Screen. It runs full-screen like an app.
  • Native app comingThe Fishtape iOS app adds an ARKit tape measure for wire runs, keeps your sign-in in the iOS Keychain and prints from every Print button. App Store listing coming.
  • Apple PencilPressure on the drawings. With a pencil, your finger pans; without one, your finger draws.

How it works on site

  1. Sign in at app.fishtapehq.com in Safari on the iPad.
  2. Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Pick metric or imperial the first time. Each person picks their own.
  4. Use it on site. It syncs when it finds signal.

Which plan includes it

Every plan has no caps on invoices, estimates, jobs, clients or drawings. Plans differ by features and included users.

  • My dayEvery plan
  • Time clockEvery plan
  • Wire runsEvery plan

Good to know. The App Store listing is being prepared. Until it's out, the web app does everything except the AR tape measure on iPhone and iPad.
