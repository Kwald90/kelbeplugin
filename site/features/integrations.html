Features / Integrations and imports

Integrations

Your accounts, connected. Your old data, brought across.

Fishtape connects to your own Stripe, email, SMS and Facebook accounts, with a setup guide and a connection test for each. Bring your clients and price book from the software you're leaving.

Included in: Every plan Compare plans

Start free trial See pricing

What it does

  • StripeCard payments on invoices through your own Stripe account. The payment is recorded on the invoice when Stripe confirms it.
  • SendGrid or ResendEstimates, invoices and follow-ups emailed from your own domain, with domain authentication so they don't land in spam.
  • TwilioTexts for follow-ups and reminders from a local number, with STOP and START handled.
  • QuickBooks Online and importsExport customers, invoices and payments as files QuickBooks Online imports. Import clients, items and jobs from Jobber, QuickBooks, Housecall Pro or any spreadsheet.

How it works on site

  1. Open Integrations and pick the service. Each has a step-by-step guide.
  2. Paste the key once. It is encrypted and never shown again; the card shows only the last few characters.
  3. Press Test to check the connection.
  4. Or ask us to do it with you: see Services.

Which plan includes it

Every plan has no caps on invoices, estimates, jobs, clients or drawings. Plans differ by features and included users.

  • Stripe invoice paymentsPro and up
  • Email (SendGrid or Resend)Pro and up
  • SMS (Twilio)Business and up
  • Facebook Lead AdsBusiness and up
  • Imports and QuickBooks exportEvery plan

Good to know. A live QuickBooks Online connection (sending invoices and payments both ways without files) is coming; today it is a CSV export in QuickBooks' import format. Keys belong to your company and are never shared with other companies.
