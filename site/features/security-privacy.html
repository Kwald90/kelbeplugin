Security and privacy

Your data stays yours, and separate

Every company's records are kept apart by the database itself, not just by the app. Owners sign in with two-step codes, keys are encrypted, and every change is written to an audit trail.

Included in: Pro and up Compare plans

What it does

  • Separate by designPostgreSQL row-level security on every company table: a request can only ever see its own company's rows. Fishtape staff access to a company is granted, time-limited and logged.
  • Sign-inPasswords hashed with argon2id, two-step codes required for owners and admins, lockouts after repeated failures, and PINs for crew on a shared iPad.
  • Keys and secretsYour Stripe, email, SMS and AI keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and never sent back to the browser.
  • Audit trail and exportEvery change is recorded with who, what and when, in a tamper-evident chain. Export your data any time; downgrading never deletes it.

How it works on site

  1. Turn on two-step sign-in when you first sign in (owners and admins must).
  2. Give each person the role they need: office, foreman, electrician, apprentice.
  3. Review who has access in the Security screen.
  4. Export a full backup whenever you like.
