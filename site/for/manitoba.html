Provincial amendments in the app

Manitoba's voltage drop rule applies to branch circuits only, and the calculator says so for Manitoba companies. Our own one-line summaries; the code book and your inspector decide.

  • Rule 2-004. Section 2 administrative rules 2-000 and 2-004 to 2-032 do not apply; Manitoba Hydro or the Winnipeg Electrical By-law administers permits.
  • Rule 6-400. Utility metering standards prevail.
  • Rule 8-102. The voltage drop limit applies to branch circuits only.
  • Rule 8-202. EV loads count at 100% in apartment calculations unless an energy management system is used.
  • Rule 10-102. Rod electrodes: two rods at least 15.8 mm in diameter, at least 3 m apart.
  • Rule 10-116. No splices in the grounding conductor except by approved methods.
  • Rule 10-210. Replaced by Manitoba's own rule.
  • Rule 12-2208. Cable tray bonded at least every 15 m.
  • Rule 26-704. GFCI on block-heater receptacles for emergency or fleet vehicles may be left off with written approval.
  • Rule 26-724. One duplex receptacle per driveway or parking area.
  • Rule 64-110. Single-phase inverters may not connect to three-phase systems.
  • Rule 76-006. Requirements for temporary service posts.
