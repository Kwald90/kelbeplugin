Provincial amendments in the app

New Brunswick's own dwelling receptacle rule (26-722) and PEI's Schedule 1 are on the checklists for companies in those provinces. Our own one-line summaries; the code book and your inspector decide.

  • New Brunswick, Rule 26-722. NB's own dwelling receptacle rule: finished rooms no point more than 1.8 m from a receptacle; kitchen counters 900 mm; islands and peninsulas 600 × 300 mm or larger need one; none directly in front of or behind the sink; hallways 4.5 m.
  • Prince Edward Island, Rule 2-036. The inspector may refuse permits to contractors with uncorrected defects.
  • Prince Edward Island, Rule 6-112. Attachment point no higher than 9 m with set clearances; masts at least 1.2 m above the roof.
  • Prince Edward Island, Rule 64-210. PV arc-fault protection wording changed.
  • Prince Edward Island, Rule 72-114. Receptacle posts in mobile home and RV parks.
